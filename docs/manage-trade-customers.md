**Trade Accounts** gives your business customers their own accounts. They sign in with an emailed link, so there are no passwords to remember.

## Add a customer

1. Open **Trade Customers** and press **Add Customer**.
2. Enter the **Company**, and optionally the **Contact name** and **Notes**.
3. Enter their **Email**. They sign in with this address. You can add a second email and a phone number.
4. Choose their **Starting status**: **Pending** or **Active**.
5. Add their address and an optional **Logo**, then **Save**.

## Change their status

Use the row actions:

- **Activate** lets them in.
- **Suspend** stops them signing in for now.
- **Close account** ends the account. Suspended and closed customers move to their own list.
- **Log in as customer** lets you see the site as they do.
- **Delete** removes them.

## Sign-in security

Press **Settings**. With **Ask for an emailed code after the login link** on, customers click the emailed link and then type a 6-digit code sent to the same address. With it off, the link alone signs them in.

## Where customers come from

**Customer source** is normally **None**, so you add and approve customers yourself. If you choose a source such as a CRM, its new applicants and approvals create and update customers here.
