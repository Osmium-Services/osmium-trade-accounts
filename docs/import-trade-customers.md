---
title: Import trade customers from a spreadsheet
tags: trade, import, csv, excel, xlsx, customers, bulk, spreadsheet
---

You can add many trade customers at once from a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file, for example an export from your old system.

1. On **Trade Customers**, choose the import.
2. **File:** choose your file, with the column headings in the first row. Only the first sheet of an Excel file is read.
3. **Columns:** say which column in your file is which customer field. A sample of each column is shown to help.
4. **Check:** review a sample of the customers. Choose whether to add them as **Active** or **Pending**.
5. Press **Import**.

Nothing is added until the final step. Customers who already exist are left alone, so it is safe to run again. No customer is dropped for missing details: a default is filled in and noted.
